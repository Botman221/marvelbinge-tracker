# 🎬 MarvelBinge Tracker

**An interactive checklist for bingeing the entire Marvel universe — from Iron Man to Avengers: Doomsday and beyond.**

Check off what you've watched, pick a viewing order, and toggle whole packs on or off (X-Men, Sony's Spider-Man, Netflix, the classic cartoons, and more). Set your target — the release you want to catch up before — and the tracker tells you what to watch next and how many hours a day you need. Your progress saves automatically in your browser.

👉 **[Open MarvelBinge Tracker](https://botman221.github.io/marvelbinge-tracker/)**

## ✨ Features

- **237 titles** — movies, series, animation, One-Shots, web shorts and documentaries, from 1944 through the 2027 slate
- **Up next** — the next title in your order, marked watched with one tap
- **Plan strip** — your whole plan as one bar: each stripe is a title, its width is the runtime
- **Three viewing orders** — recommended, by release, and chronological
- **Weighted packs** — every pack is essential, worth it or “someday”; by default the plan shows only the essentials (237 h to Doomsday instead of 1,500+), and the rest is one tap away
- **Doomsday focus** — a shorter path through the key titles (★) or Disney+'s official “Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday” list
- **Search** — by original or Polish title, with or without diacritics
- **Collapsible sections** with progress bars and a checkbox that marks a whole section at once
- **Your pace** — worked out from the last two weeks, with a likely finish date
- **Countdown to any upcoming Marvel premiere** or a custom date
- **Spoiler-safe notes** — hidden by default; reveal them one at a time
- **Undo** for every checkmark, plus a backup file and a link that moves your progress to another device
- **Light and dark theme**, two languages (English and Polish — toggle in the corner, or `?lang=en` in the URL)
- Single HTML file: no install, no cookies, no third-party requests, works offline
- Progress saved by earlier versions carries over automatically

## 🚀 Run it yourself

Download `index.html` and open it in your browser — that's it. To host your own copy online, drop the file on GitHub Pages, Netlify, or any static host.
